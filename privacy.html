Last updated: June 11, 2026

JJ Shorts ("we", "our", or "us") operates the JJ Shorts web application (the "Service"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you use our Service, including:

  • TikTok account information (access tokens) for the purpose of publishing videos
  • Video files you choose to upload through our Service
  • API keys you enter for third-party services (stored locally in your browser only)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Publish video content to TikTok and other platforms on your behalf

3. Data Storage

All API keys and tokens are stored locally in your browser's localStorage. We do not store your credentials on any external server. Video files are temporarily processed and uploaded to the platform of your choice.

4. Third-Party Services

Our Service integrates with the following third-party platforms:

  • TikTok (Content Posting API)
  • YouTube (YouTube Data API v3)
  • Google Drive (for video hosting)
  • Google Gemini AI (for content analysis)

Each platform's use of your data is governed by their respective privacy policies.

5. Data Retention

We do not retain your personal data beyond the immediate session. Access tokens stored in localStorage are under your control and can be cleared at any time.

6. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
